# Law n° 1/25 of 4 december 2018 portaining to the ratification of the East African Community protocol on peace and security by the Republic of Burundi.

REPUBLIC OF BURUNDI OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT LAW N°1/ À S OF l DECEMBER 2018 PERTAINING TO THE RATIFICATION OF THE EAST AFRICAN COMMUNITY PROTOCOL ON PEACE AND SECURITY BY THE REPUBLIC CF BURUNDI THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC, Given the Constitution of the Republic; Noting the provisions of the Treaty on the establishment of the East African Community; Considering the East African Community Protocol on Peace and Security; Whereas the Council of Ministers have considered; And Whereas the National Assembly and Senate have adopted; PROMULGATES AS FOLLOWS:

## Article 1

The Republic of Burundi ratifies the East African Community Protocol on Peace and Security.

## Article 2

This Act Shall come into force on the day of its promulgation. Done at Bujumbura, on de december 2018.
